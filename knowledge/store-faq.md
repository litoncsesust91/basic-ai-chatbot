# Demo Store Knowledge Base

## Store information

Demo Store is an online retailer based in Bangladesh.
All prices are displayed in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT).

## Delivery

Standard delivery normally takes 2–5 business days.

Delivery inside Dhaka costs BDT 80.
Delivery outside Dhaka costs BDT 150.

Orders above BDT 5,000 receive free standard delivery.

## Returns

Customers may request a return within 14 days of delivery.

Returned products must be unused, unwashed, and in their
original packaging.

Personalized products and gift cards cannot be returned.

Customers should contact support@example.com before sending
a product back.
